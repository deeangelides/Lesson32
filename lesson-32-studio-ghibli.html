Meet Studio Ghibli

Studio Ghibli is one of the most beloved animation studios in the world. Based in Japan, it is famous for making beautiful, hand-drawn films that are filled with magic, adventure and heart. For nearly forty years, its stories have enchanted children and grown-ups all over the globe.

🔍 Word clue: enchanted = filled someone with magic and delight. ✨

🌿 the sign hidden in the leaves

How it began

Studio Ghibli was founded in 1985 by two talented directors, Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata, together with their producer, Toshio Suzuki. The name “Ghibli” comes from an old word for a warm desert wind. Miyazaki chose it because he wanted the studio to blow a fresh new wind through the world of animation — and it certainly did.

Hand-drawn magic

At a time when many studios were switching to computers, Studio Ghibli kept drawing its films largely by hand. Every frame is painted with astonishing care, from blades of rustling grass to bubbling pots of food. This loving attention to detail is one reason the films feel so alive and so real, even when they are full of magic.

Stories with heart

Ghibli films are known for their brave young heroes — often ordinary girls who show great courage. Rather than simple goodies and baddies, the stories explore kindness, nature and the adventure of growing up. Films such as My Neighbour Totoro, Kiki's Delivery Service and Spirited Away have become famous around the world. In 2003, Spirited Away even won an Academy Award, one of the highest honours in all of film.

🔍 Word clue: courage = being brave when something is hard or scary. 🦁

A lasting wind

Today, the studio's friendly forest spirit, Totoro, is known everywhere as its gentle symbol. There is even a Ghibli Museum in Tokyo, where fans can step inside the magical worlds of the films. More than thirty years after it began, Studio Ghibli is still blowing its fresh, imaginative wind across the world of animation.

🔍 Word clue: symbol = a picture or sign that stands for something bigger. 🌳

🔁 New Skill · Section 3

Two kinds of word pairs

Synonym 🟰

A word that means the same (or almost the same) as another word.

beloved=loved

Antonym ↔️

A word that means the opposite of another word.

friendly≠unfriendly

💡 Memory trick: Synonym = Same · Antonym = Against

✏️ New Skill · Section 4

Past participles

The past participle is the verb form we use after has, have or had: “Ghibli has drawn many films.”

Regular verbs 😊

Just add -ed
paint → has painted · watch → has watched

Irregular verbs 🤪

They change in their own way — learn them!
draw → has drawn · win → has won
